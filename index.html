How it works. Annual bonus = sum assured ÷ 1000 × bonus rate. Total bonus = annual bonus × years. Maturity amount = sum assured + total bonus. Net premium = premium − rebate + tax.

Premium accuracy. Rates for entry age 24 come from your quote and are exact. For other entry ages the premium is estimated by interpolating those rates by policy duration, so it can differ from the official PLI table. Check the official PLI premium table or your post office before buying.

Approx. return is the annualised return on the premiums paid, ignoring the life cover and any tax benefit. Bonus rate shown is the commonly published Rs. 52 per Rs. 1000 for endowment; edit it if the declared rate changes. This is an estimate, not an official quote.
